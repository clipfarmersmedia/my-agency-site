Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Clip Farmers Media ("we", "us") is a short-form video agency serving New Jersey and New York City. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us: your name, email, business name, selected tier and message when you use our contact form or email us.
  • Analytics data (only if you accept cookies): pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and referral source, collected through Google Analytics.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider (Netlify) and form service process standard server logs such as IP address and request time for security and delivery.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide quotes or services.
  • To understand and improve how the website performs.
  • To protect the site against spam and abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

Analytics cookies are off by default. They load only after you click "Accept" in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. Your choice is stored in your browser.

Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help run the site: Netlify (hosting and form handling), Google (analytics, if you consent), and our email provider. They may only use it to provide their services. We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry messages for as long as needed to respond and maintain a business relationship, then delete them within a reasonable period. Analytics data is retained according to our analytics settings.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and opt out of analytics at any time. Depending on where you live (including New Jersey and other U.S. states), you may have additional privacy rights. Contact us at the email below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
